DRE — Africa Multiple Digital Research Environment vocabulary

Properties the Africa Multiple Digital Research Environment adds to Dublin Core, BIBO, FOAF and the other vocabularies of AMIRA, the Africa Multiple Interactive Research Atlas: identifiers and links into the University of Bayreuth's systems, provenance of machine-generated values, and the internal fields that keep AMIRA in sync with its source database.

Former namespace

Until 2026-10-15 these terms were published under http://am-digital.org/ontology/dre/, which never resolved. Each term here is declared owl:equivalentProperty to its former IRI, so data loaded from earlier dumps can still be joined with current data.

Collection URL dre:collectionUrl

https://data.africamultiple.uni-bayreuth.de/ontology/dre#collectionUrl

Permalink to the item in the University of Bayreuth Digital Collections (DSpace) platform at collections.uni-bayreuth.de. A different system from RDSpace.

Equivalent to the former IRI http://am-digital.org/ontology/dre/collectionUrl.

Generated by dre:generatedBy

https://data.africamultiple.uni-bayreuth.de/ontology/dre#generatedBy

The agent — typically an AI model — that automatically generated this value (e.g. a machine-produced transcript, description or translation). Used as a value annotation pointing to the generating tool's authority record, distinguishing machine-generated content from a human contributor role.

Equivalent to the former IRI http://am-digital.org/ontology/dre/generatedBy.

Series dre:series

https://data.africamultiple.uni-bayreuth.de/ontology/dre#series

The bibliographic series (and series number) a publication belongs to, e.g. 'BIGSASworks!; 23'. Kept distinct from dcterms:isPartOf, which holds the publication's single immediate container (its journal or book) — a monograph or working paper with no such container uses its primary series as the isPartOf container instead, and only a secondary series statement lands here.

Equivalent to the former IRI http://am-digital.org/ontology/dre/series.

WissKI URL dre:wisskiUrldeprecated

https://data.africamultiple.uni-bayreuth.de/ontology/dre#wisskiUrl

Navigate URL to the item in the WissKI knowledge base at the University of Bayreuth. No longer populated: WissKI links were retired from the sync.

Equivalent to the former IRI http://am-digital.org/ontology/dre/wisskiUrl.
